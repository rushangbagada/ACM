# ACM SVNIT Cyber-Professional Website

## Core Philosophy
A high-performance, award-winning "Cyber-Professional" aesthetic. Deep, dark, and sleek without being cartoonish. 

## Colors
- Primary Background: Matte Black / Deep Charcoal (#0a0a0a)
- Primary Accent: Electric Blue (#0070f3)
- Secondary Accent: Silver / Metallic Grey (#c0c0c0)
- Surface/Card: Glassmorphism / Translucent dark panels with subtle white borders.
- Text Primary: Pure White or slightly off-white (#f0f0f0)
- Text Secondary: Silver/Grey (#a0a0a0)

## Typography
- Font Family: Inter
- Headings: Bold, high-end typography.

## UI Elements
- Buttons: Magnetic feel, sharp corners with subtle border radius.
- Cards: Glassmorphism, translucent backgrounds.
